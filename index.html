Compatibility and notes

    Known E36 problems to check on any build

    • Rear subframe mounts: the mounting points can crack or tear from the chassis, especially with more power, sticky tires or track use. Inspect and reinforce them.
    • Cooling system: plastic radiator necks, expansion tank, thermostat housing and water pump fail with age.
    • Oil leaks: valve cover, oil filter housing and oil pan gaskets are common leak points.
    • VANOS seals: worn seals cause a rattle and power loss on M52 and S52 engines.
    • Bushings and mounts: rear trailing arm bushings, front control arm bushings and front strut guide supports wear out and hurt handling.
    • Rust: check the rear arches, sills and jacking points on any car before you start.
